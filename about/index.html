About the Law Firm Ad Library

A free research tool from Rocket Launch Media for law firms that want to see what others are running.

Browse all ads

What is in the library

  • 2,504 ads and 1,054 unique creatives from 64 law firms.
  • Ads from US immigration law firms on Facebook and Instagram, plus a small set from other platforms.
  • Tags for case type, language, format, hook style and where the ad sends people.

Where the ads come from

Ads come from the Meta Ad Library, including data collected through Foreplay. We group near-identical versions into one creative and check new ads for duplicates.

What it does not show

There is no spend, lead or results data. A long run time can be a useful signal, but it does not prove an ad worked. Case tags and some hook tags are assigned automatically, so a few may be off.

Remove an ad

If you represent an advertiser and want an ad removed, contact Rocket Launch Media and include the firm name and ad link.
