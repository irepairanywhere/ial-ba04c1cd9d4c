Questions

What is the Law Firm Ad Library?

It is a free library of 2,504 real ads from 64 law firms, focused on US immigration firms on Facebook and Instagram.

Is it free?

Yes. You can browse, filter and swipe through the ads for free.

Where do the ads come from?

They come from the Meta Ad Library, including data collected through Foreplay.

Does it show spend or results?

No. There is no spend or results data. Run time is shown as a signal to study.

Can a firm ask to remove an ad?

Yes. Use the contact details on the About page.
